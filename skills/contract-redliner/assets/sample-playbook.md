# Sample Legal Playbook — Acme Corp

This is a complete example playbook for reference. Use it as a model when building your own. This playbook reflects the standards of a mid-market technology company ($5M revenue, 40 employees).

---

**Company Name:** Acme Corp
**Last Updated:** March 2026
**Approved By:** General Counsel
**Review Schedule:** Annually (January)

---

## 1. Standard Acceptable Terms

| Term | Our Standard | Notes |
|------|-------------|-------|
| Mutual NDA | Mutual obligations, 2-3 year survival period | Accept any reasonable mutual NDA |
| Confidentiality | Mutual, reasonable scope, excludes publicly available info | Standard in all commercial contracts |
| Payment terms | Net 30 | Net 15 also acceptable |
| Governing law | Delaware or New York | Our incorporation state is Delaware |
| Termination notice | 30 days written notice, mutual | Both parties should have equal exit rights |
| Force majeure | Standard mutual clause | Accept as written in most cases |
| Dispute resolution | Mediation first, then litigation in Delaware | Prefer litigation over arbitration |
| Assignment | Neither party may assign without consent | Standard anti-assignment clause |
| Warranties | Mutual representations of authority and legal standing | Boilerplate, accept as written |

---

## 2. Unacceptable Terms (Hard Stops)

| Term | Why | Required Alternative |
|------|-----|---------------------|
| Unlimited liability for Acme | Unlimited financial exposure | Mutual cap at 12 months' fees |
| Vendor owns Acme's custom work product | Loss of IP we paid for | Acme owns custom deliverables upon payment |
| Vendor can terminate without cause, Acme cannot | One-sided exit rights | Mutual termination for convenience |
| Non-mutual indemnification (Acme indemnifies, vendor does not) | One-sided risk | Mutual indemnification for breach and negligence |
| Vendor owns or can use Acme's data for any purpose | Privacy and competitive risk | Acme retains all data rights, vendor use limited to service delivery |
| Mandatory arbitration outside Delaware/New York | Unfavorable venue | Litigation in Delaware or mutual agreement on venue |
| Automatic price increases over 5% without consent | Uncapped cost escalation | Increases require written agreement or cap at CPI |

---

## 3. Negotiable Terms

| Term | Our Preference | When Alternatives Are Acceptable |
|------|---------------|--------------------------------|
| Payment terms | Net 30 | Net 45-60 for enterprise vendors with significant leverage |
| Liability cap | 1x annual contract value | Up to 2x for mission-critical services |
| Auto-renewal notice | 30 days to opt out | 60 days acceptable for contracts under $50K/year |
| Non-compete | Not applicable (we are the customer) | Narrow scope, max 12 months, for vendor-side contracts |
| SLA uptime | 99.9% with monthly service credits | 99.5% acceptable for non-critical services |
| Insurance | Standard commercial general liability | Higher limits for on-site work or data handling |
| Audit rights | Once per year, 30 days' notice | Twice per year acceptable for compliance-sensitive services |
| Data retention | Delete within 30 days of termination | 90 days acceptable if documented in DPA |

## 4. Escalation Rules

| Situation | Escalate To | Response Time |
|-----------|------------|---------------|
| Any red flag | General Counsel | Within 2 business days |
| Contract value over $100K annually | CFO + General Counsel | Before signing |
| Non-standard IP terms | General Counsel (IP focus) | Within 3 business days |
| Data processing / GDPR / CCPA terms | General Counsel + VP Engineering | Within 2 business days |
| Employment or contractor classification | HR Director + General Counsel | Within 2 business days |
| International jurisdiction | General Counsel | Within 3 business days |

## 5. Standard Language

**Limitation of Liability:**
"Neither party's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall exceed the total fees paid or payable by Customer to Vendor during the twelve (12) month period immediately preceding the event giving rise to the claim. This limitation shall not apply to breaches of confidentiality obligations or indemnification obligations."

**Termination for Convenience:**
"Either party may terminate this Agreement for any reason upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice to the other party. Upon termination, Vendor shall provide Customer with all work product completed to date and a prorated refund of any prepaid fees for the unused portion of the term."

**IP Ownership:**
"All work product created by Vendor specifically for Customer under this Agreement ('Custom Deliverables') shall be owned by Customer upon full payment. Vendor retains all rights to pre-existing materials, tools, and general knowledge ('Vendor Materials'). To the extent Vendor Materials are incorporated into Custom Deliverables, Vendor grants Customer a perpetual, non-exclusive license to use such Vendor Materials solely as part of the Custom Deliverables."

**Data Ownership:**
"Customer retains all rights, title, and interest in and to Customer Data. Vendor shall not use Customer Data for any purpose other than performing the Services under this Agreement. Upon termination, Vendor shall return or destroy all Customer Data within thirty (30) days and provide written certification of destruction."

---

## 6. Required Clauses

Flag if any of these are missing from a contract:

- [ ] Limitation of liability (must be mutual)
- [ ] Confidentiality / NDA provisions
- [ ] Termination provisions (both for cause and convenience)
- [ ] Data ownership, handling, and deletion
- [ ] IP ownership for custom work
- [ ] Governing law and dispute resolution
- [ ] Indemnification (must be mutual)
- [ ] Assignment restrictions
- [ ] Data Processing Agreement (if personal data is involved)
- [ ] SLA with service credits (for SaaS and hosted services)
