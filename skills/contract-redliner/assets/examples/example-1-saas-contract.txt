SAAS SOFTWARE LICENSE AGREEMENT

This SaaS Software License Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into
as of April 1, 2026 ("Effective Date") by and between:

CloudMetrics Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Vendor")
and
Acme Corp, a Delaware corporation ("Customer")

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Services" means the cloud-based analytics platform provided
by Vendor, including data visualization, reporting, and dashboard
functionality.

1.2 "Customer Data" means all data uploaded, entered, or generated
by Customer through use of the Services.

1.3 "Authorized Users" means individuals authorized by Customer to
access and use the Services, limited to the number of seats
purchased.

2. SERVICES AND ACCESS

2.1 License Grant. Vendor grants Customer a non-exclusive,
non-transferable right to access and use the Services during the
Term, subject to the terms of this Agreement.

2.2 Authorized Users. Customer may authorize up to fifty (50)
users to access the Services. Additional seats may be purchased
at Vendor's then-current rates.

2.3 Restrictions. Customer shall not (a) sublicense the Services,
(b) reverse engineer or decompile the Services, (c) use the
Services to build a competing product, or (d) exceed the authorized
number of users.

3. TERM AND RENEWAL

3.1 Initial Term. This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and
continues for twelve (12) months ("Initial Term").

3.2 Renewal. This Agreement shall automatically renew for
successive twelve (12) month periods unless either party provides
written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days prior to the
end of the then-current term.

4. FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 Fees. Customer shall pay Vendor $4,000 per month for 50 seats
($48,000 annually). Fees are invoiced monthly in advance.

4.2 Payment Terms. All invoices are due within thirty (30) days of
the invoice date.

4.3 Late Payments. Overdue amounts shall accrue interest at 1.5%
per month or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is less.

4.4 Taxes. Fees are exclusive of all taxes. Customer is responsible
for all applicable taxes.

5. DATA

5.1 Customer Data Ownership. Customer retains all rights, title,
and interest in Customer Data.

5.2 Data Security. Vendor shall implement and maintain reasonable
administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect
Customer Data.

5.3 Data Usage. Vendor may use Customer Data in anonymized,
aggregated form for any business purpose, including but not limited
to product improvement, benchmarking, and marketing materials.

5.4 Data Return. Upon termination, Vendor shall make Customer Data
available for download for thirty (30) days. After such period,
Vendor may delete Customer Data.

6. COMPLIANCE AND AUDIT

6.1 Usage Compliance. Customer shall use the Services in compliance
with all applicable laws and this Agreement.

6.2 Vendor Monitoring. Vendor may monitor Customer's usage of the
Services to verify compliance with the authorized number of users.

6.3 Audit Rights. Vendor may audit Customer's use of the Services
at any time upon five (5) business days' written notice to verify
compliance with the terms of this Agreement.

7. CONFIDENTIALITY

7.1 Definition. "Confidential Information" means any non-public
information disclosed by either party that is designated as
confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be
confidential.

7.2 Obligations. Each party shall protect the other's Confidential
Information using the same degree of care it uses for its own
confidential information, but no less than reasonable care.

7.3 Exceptions. Confidential Information does not include
information that (a) is or becomes publicly available, (b) was
known prior to disclosure, (c) is independently developed, or
(d) is received from a third party without restriction.

7.4 Survival. Confidentiality obligations survive for two (2) years
after termination.

8. INDEMNIFICATION

8.1 Vendor Indemnification. Vendor shall indemnify and defend
Customer against third-party claims alleging that the Services
infringe any intellectual property right.

8.2 Customer Indemnification. Customer shall indemnify and defend
Vendor against any and all claims, damages, losses, and expenses
arising from Customer's use of the Services, including claims by
Customer's end users or data subjects.

9. TERMINATION

9.1 Termination for Cause. Either party may terminate this
Agreement upon thirty (30) days' written notice if the other
party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure within
the notice period.

9.2 Effect of Termination. Upon termination, Customer's access
to the Services shall cease. Sections 5, 7, 8, 10, and 12
survive termination.

10. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

10.1 Vendor Liability Cap. Vendor's aggregate liability under
this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid by Customer
during the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

10.2 Customer Liability. CUSTOMER'S LIABILITY UNDER THIS AGREEMENT
SHALL NOT BE LIMITED.

10.3 Exclusion of Damages. NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE FOR
INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, OR PUNITIVE
DAMAGES.

11. FORCE MAJEURE

Neither party shall be liable for delays or failures in
performance resulting from causes beyond its reasonable control,
including natural disasters, war, terrorism, labor disputes,
government actions, or failures of third-party services.

12. GENERAL PROVISIONS

12.1 Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of
the State of Delaware.

12.2 Dispute Resolution. Any disputes shall be resolved through
good-faith negotiation, followed by mediation, and if unresolved,
litigation in the courts of Delaware.

12.3 Entire Agreement. This Agreement constitutes the entire
agreement between the parties.

12.4 Amendment. This Agreement may only be amended in writing
signed by both parties.

12.5 Assignment. Neither party may assign this Agreement without
the other party's prior written consent.
