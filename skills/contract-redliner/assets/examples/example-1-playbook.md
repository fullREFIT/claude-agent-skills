# Playbook Used for Example 1

The example SaaS contract review in SKILL.md uses the full Sample Playbook
from `assets/sample-playbook.md` (Acme Corp's legal playbook).

Key playbook standards applied:

- Liability cap: Mutual, 1x annual contract value
- Auto-renewal notice: 30 days preferred
- Indemnification: Must be mutual
- Data ownership: Customer retains all rights, vendor use limited to service delivery
- Audit rights: Once per year, 30 days' notice
- Termination: Both for cause and convenience, mutual
- Required clauses: DPA if personal data, SLA with service credits

See `assets/sample-playbook.md` for the complete playbook.
